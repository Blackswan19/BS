is the place we living in really called “earth,” or was that just a random name given by some 'great scientists'(they call) based on an imaginary subject created by an imaginary person? we don’t even know what we’re actually living on. we simply give names to things and follow the meanings and values attached to those names. we create systems, rules, ranks, marks, money, gold, and silver, and then spend our whole lives chasing them. we create these things, give them value, build our lives around them, and sometimes even fight over them. but who decided that a rank means success? who decided that marks mean intelligence? who decided that money, gold, or silver have value? are we just living in a world of names, rules, and meanings that we created ourselves, and then forgot that we were the ones who created them? are we actually living the way we were designed to—or simply the way we designed life to be?
